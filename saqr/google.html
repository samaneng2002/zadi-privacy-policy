Privacy Policy for Saqr

Effective date: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains what Saqr ("the game", "we", "us") does with information when you play the Google Play version. Saqr is made by EasyMeasy ("the developer"). Questions go to ahmed.saman11@gmail.com.

In one paragraph: Saqr keeps your progress on your own device, has no accounts and no server of ours, and shows optional ads and sells optional items through Google Play. The advertising and payment services of Google Play see the technical information they need to do that. We ask you for nothing else, and we cannot identify you from anything the game collects.

2. Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (unlocked heroes, weapon levels, currency, settings, language, daily quests and streaks, the local leaderboard, which ads you have already watched today) is written to a save file inside the game's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us. We do not operate a server that could receive it.

Uninstalling the game deletes the save. If Android's automatic backup is turned on for your device, a copy of the save may also be kept in your own Google account's backup (or the equivalent on your device); you control that backup, and we have no access to it.

3. Information collected by the advertising service

The game shows rewarded and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure an ad, that service automatically collects and receives, from your device:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (the game does not ask for your precise location and has no location permission);
  • device and advertising identifiers: the Google advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • interactions with the ads (that one was shown, tapped, or watched to the end);
  • technical and diagnostic information (device model, system version, app version, performance and crash data).

This information is sent securely (encrypted in transit). It is collected and used by the advertising provider under its own privacy policy, as an independent data controller. We do not receive it, and we do not combine it with anything else.

4. Ads and your choices

Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Saqr shows Google's consent form the first time you open the game. Ads are personalized only if you agree there. If you decline, or close the form without choosing, only non-personalized ads are shown.

Elsewhere, Google's standard settings apply. You can reopen the choice at any time from Settings → Manage ad privacy in the game.

You can also control advertising yourself:

  • In the game: Settings → Manage ad privacy.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (Android 13 and later), or Opt out of Ads Personalization on earlier versions. The game and its ad provider then see a blank or new identifier.
  • Google Ad Settings: adssettings.google.com and myadcenter.google.com, while signed in to your Google account, show and let you delete advertising data Google holds.

5. Purchases

The game offers optional purchases (scrap bundles, cosmetics, a hero pack, a season pass and Remove Ads) through Google Play Billing. The whole payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your name, card or account details. The game learns only which product was bought, so that it can unlock it in your save on your device. Nothing in the game is random-chance paid content, and everything can also be earned by playing.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game may schedule a local reminder on your device, for example about a daily streak, using Android's own alarm system. This involves no network request, no push service and no server of ours. You can turn notifications off at any time in Android's per-app settings.

7. What the game does not do

  • It has no account, login, profile or chat, and no user-generated content.
  • It has no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of our own.
  • It does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or phone number.
  • It does not sell your information. (Some laws describe the advertising provider's own use of advertising identifiers as a "sale" or "sharing"; you can opt out as described in section 4.)

8. Why information is used, and your rights

The advertising service uses the information in section 3 to show ads, to measure them, and to prevent fraud and abuse. That is the only purpose. Where the law requires your consent for personalized ads (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), it is asked for as described in section 4.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal information, to take it with you, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you, the data these rights apply to is held by the advertising provider: use the controls in section 4, or write to us and we will help you reach them. We will answer within a reasonable time.

9. Retention, security and transfers

We keep no personal information, so there is nothing for us to retain or secure on a server. Your save stays on your device until you delete it. Data sent to the advertising provider is encrypted in transit; how long the provider keeps it, and where in the world it is processed, is set out in the provider's own policy, linked at the end of this page.

10. Children

Saqr is an action game for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child's information has reached us in a way this policy does not describe, write to ahmed.saman11@gmail.com and we will look into it.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and for a change that matters the game's next update will say so in its release notes. Please check this page now and then.
