Privacy Policy for Zadi: Athan, Qibla & Prayer Times

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Zadi: Athan, Qibla & Prayer Times ("Zadi", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how EasyMeasy ("Developer") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using Zadi, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Location Information: Zadi requires access to your device's precise or approximate location data to accurately calculate Islamic prayer times (Athan) and determine the direction of the Qibla based on your geographical position.
  • Device and Network Information: We collect device-specific information such as your hardware model, operating system version, device identifiers (including Huawei OAID or Google Advertising ID – GAID), screen resolution, network type, and IP address.
  • App Usage Data: We collect crash reports, performance data, and analytics regarding how you interact with the app features (such as clicks, app open times, and ad video plays) to improve functionality and resolve bugs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To deliver core app functionality, specifically accurate prayer schedules and Qibla compass tracking.
  • To serve contextual and personalized advertisements through third-party ad networks.
  • To analyze app performance, detect fraud, and improve the user experience.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Zadi uses third-party advertising services to display ads and, where enabled, to personalize them:

Huawei Petal Ads (Huawei AppGallery version)

  • We share relevant data, such as device identifiers (Huawei OAID) and approximate location (if location-based ads are permitted), with Petal Ads. All data transmitted to Petal Ads is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Huawei Privacy Policy: consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy
  • Petal Ads Statement: Petal Ads Privacy Statement

Google AdMob (where applicable)

  • We share device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and, where location-based ads are enabled, approximate location data with Google AdMob to serve advertisements. All data transmitted to Google AdMob is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Ad Settings (manage or delete your advertising data): adssettings.google.com

5. How to Manage, Disable, or Delete Your Advertising Data

You have the right to opt out of personalized advertising and to request deletion of the advertising-related data associated with your device. You can do this in the following ways:

  • Reset your Huawei OAID (Huawei / HMS devices): Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads and privacy (or Settings > HMS Core > Ads, depending on your OS version) and select Disable personalized ads. Your OAID will be replaced with a non-unique, all-zero value.
  • Delete your Google Advertising ID (GMS devices): Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (Android 13 and above) or Opt out of Ads Personalization (earlier versions). This immediately deletes or resets the identifier used to associate advertising data with your device.
  • Manage or delete data via Google Ad Settings: Visit adssettings.google.com (or Google's My Ad Center at myadcenter.google.com) while signed in to your Google Account.
  • Disable location-based ads: You can revoke Zadi's location permission at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > Zadi > Permissions > Location, or disable ads personalization as described above.
  • Request manual deletion: For any other data deletion request, or if you have questions about data we may hold, contact us directly at zadi.muslim.app@gmail.com and we will respond to and action your request within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Data Retention and Security

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide Zadi's services and to comply with legal obligations. Zadi does not transmit your precise GPS location to our own servers for storage; location calculations for prayer times and Qibla direction are performed locally on your device. Any data that is transmitted to third-party advertising networks (Google AdMob, Huawei Petal Ads) – such as device identifiers or approximate location used for ad personalization – is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect device identifiers and usage data from unauthorized access.

7. Children's Privacy

Zadi does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If an ad request is labeled for child users, our ad partners will not collect any personal data or place personalized ads for children; only non-personalized ads will be delivered. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete the data.
