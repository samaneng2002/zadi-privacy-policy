Privacy Policy for KineoFit

Google Play version · Last updated: November 4, 2026

العربية

1. Who we are

KineoFit is a training and nutrition app made by EasyMeasy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Google Play version of KineoFit handles, where it goes and the choices you have. KineoFit has no user accounts and no KineoFit servers: almost everything you enter stays on your phone.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your workouts, training plan, sets and weights, runs and routes, readiness check-ins, food and water logs, body measurements, body-composition scans, progress photos, voice notes, coach conversations and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive them. Progress photos are never included in cloud backups. You can export your data to a file (JSON / CSV) and import it again from Settings > Your data; the file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

3. Camera, microphone and location

  • Camera is used only when you start it: scanning a food barcode, a meal photo estimate, reading a body-composition printout, and the camera form check / live coach. Images are analysed on your phone and are not stored or sent anywhere. Progress photos are saved only when you take them, in the app's private storage.
  • Microphone is used only while you hold the voice button (workout voice commands, speaking to the coach). Speech is turned into text on your phone when the phone supports on-device recognition or the downloaded AI model can do it; otherwise your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google's) may process the audio under its own policy. Recordings are not stored by KineoFit.
  • Location (precise) is used only while you record a run that you started, to draw the route and measure distance. The route is stored on your phone only. Location is never used for ads by KineoFit.

4. Health data (Health Connect)

If you allow it, KineoFit reads activity and health data from Health Connect (for example workouts, steps, distance, heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, weight and body fat) to adapt your training plan, calculate your daily readiness and show your progress. If you turn on each option, KineoFit writes your workouts, runs, weight, body fat, food energy and water to Health Connect so other apps can show them. You can change these permissions at any time in Health Connect.

Health data read from Health Connect is processed only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending. The use and transfer of this data follows the Health Connect permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. On-device AI

Meal-photo estimates and, on some phones, voice recognition run on an AI model that you choose to download (Gemma, from Hugging Face). The download sends no personal data; like any download, the server sees your IP address. After that, the model runs entirely on your phone: your photos and voice are not sent to us or to an AI provider. AI estimates can be wrong and are not medical advice. Coach chat answers come from the app itself, not from this model.

6. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob with Google's User Messaging Platform (consent). The ad provider may collect the Google Advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information and ad interactions to show, measure and protect ads. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree in the consent screen; you can change your choice in Settings. KineoFit never passes your health, nutrition or body data to an ad provider. Buying KineoFit Pro removes all ads.

More information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from partner apps · Ad settings. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads (delete or reset the advertising ID).

7. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. KineoFit receives only the purchase confirmation needed to unlock Pro; your payment details stay with the store.

8. Other network use

When you scan a barcode that is not in the built-in food list, the app asks Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) for that product, sending only the barcode number. Android's own backup may include KineoFit's small settings file (not your training, nutrition, body data or photos) if backup is on for your phone. On Google Play devices the app also stores the start date of the 14-day AI trial in Google Block Store, so reinstalling the app does not restart the trial. Only that date is stored there. If you turn on heat warnings, the app asks Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for today's weather at the city you picked, sending only that city's coordinates. KineoFit does not use analytics or crash-reporting services at this time; if that changes, this policy will be updated first and such services will never receive health values, measurements, photos or text you typed.

9. Children

KineoFit is meant for people aged 13 and over (16 where local law requires) and asks for your age during setup. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Keeping or deleting your data

Because your data is on your phone, you control it: delete entries in the app, use Settings > Your data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Data written to Health Connect or Huawei Health Kit can be deleted there. For ad data, use the ad settings above.

11. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage protected by Android. Network connections made by the app and its ad and purchase services use encryption (HTTPS/TLS). No method is perfectly secure, but we keep data on your device to keep the risk low.

12. Health disclaimer

KineoFit gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Stop if you feel pain and talk to a health professional before major changes.

13. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes are also shown in the app.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: kineofit.support@gmail.com

3. الكاميرا والميكروفون والموقع

  • الكاميرا تُستخدم فقط عندما تشغّلها: مسح باركود طعام، تقدير وجبة من صورة، قراءة ورقة تحليل تكوين الجسم، وفحص الأداء بالكاميرا / المدرب المباشر. تُحلَّل الصور على هاتفك ولا تُحفظ ولا تُرسل إلى أي جهة. تُحفظ صور التقدم فقط عندما تلتقطها، في التخزين الخاص بالتطبيق.

  • الموقع (الدقيق) يُستخدم فقط أثناء تسجيل جري بدأته أنت، لرسم المسار وقياس المسافة. يُحفظ المسار على هاتفك فقط. ولا يستخدم KineoFit الموقع للإعلانات أبدًا.

4. البيانات الصحية (Health Connect)

إذا سمحت بذلك، يقرأ KineoFit بيانات النشاط والصحة من Health Connect (مثل التمارين والخطوات والمسافة ونبض القلب وتغيّر معدل النبض ونبض الراحة والنوم والوزن ونسبة الدهون) لتكييف خطة تدريبك وحساب جاهزيتك اليومية وعرض تقدّمك. وإذا فعّلت كل خيار على حدة، يكتب KineoFit تمارينك وجرياتك ووزنك ونسبة الدهون وطاقة الطعام والماء إلى Health Connect لتعرضها التطبيقات الأخرى. يمكنك تغيير هذه الأذونات في أي وقت من Health Connect.

تُعالَج البيانات الصحية المقروءة من Health Connect على جهازك فقط. لا تُرسل إلينا ولا إلى أي طرف ثالث، ولا تُستخدم للإعلانات، ولا تُباع، ولا تُستخدم لتقييم الأهلية الائتمانية أو للإقراض. ويخضع استخدام هذه البيانات ونقلها لسياسة أذونات Health Connect، بما في ذلك متطلبات الاستخدام المحدود.

5. الذكاء الاصطناعي على الجهاز

يعمل تقدير الوجبات من الصور، والتعرّف على الصوت في بعض الهواتف، على نموذج ذكاء اصطناعي تختار تنزيله (Gemma من Hugging Face). لا يرسل التنزيل أي بيانات شخصية؛ وكأي تنزيل يرى الخادم عنوان IP الخاص بك. بعد ذلك يعمل النموذج بالكامل على هاتفك: لا تُرسل صورك وصوتك إلينا ولا إلى أي مزوّد ذكاء اصطناعي. قد تكون تقديرات الذكاء الاصطناعي خاطئة، وهي ليست نصيحة طبية. أما إجابات محادثة المدرب فتأتي من التطبيق نفسه، لا من هذا النموذج.

6. الإعلانات

تعرض النسخة المجانية إعلانات من Google AdMob مع منصة رسائل المستخدم من Google (الموافقة). قد يجمع مزوّد الإعلانات معرّف إعلانات Google والموقع التقريبي المستنتج من عنوان IP ومعلومات الجهاز وتفاعلاتك مع الإعلانات لعرض الإعلانات وقياسها وحمايتها. لا تُعرض الإعلانات المخصّصة إلا إذا وافقت في شاشة الموافقة، ويمكنك تغيير اختيارك من الإعدادات. لا يمرّر KineoFit أبدًا بياناتك الصحية أو الغذائية أو قياسات جسمك إلى مزوّد الإعلانات. شراء KineoFit Pro يزيل جميع الإعلانات.

لمزيد من المعلومات: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from partner apps · Ad settings. يمكنك إعادة تعيين معرّف الإعلانات أو الحدّ من تخصيص الإعلانات من إعدادات Android ← Google ← الإعلانات (حذف معرّف الإعلانات أو إعادة تعيينه).
