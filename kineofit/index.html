Privacy policy

1. Who we are

KineoFit is a training and nutrition app made by EasyMeasy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Google Play and Huawei AppGallery versions of KineoFit handle, where it goes and the choices you have. KineoFit has no user accounts and no KineoFit servers: almost everything you enter stays on your phone.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your workouts, training plan, sets and weights, runs and routes, readiness check-ins, food and water logs, body measurements, body-composition scans, progress photos, voice notes, coach conversations and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive them. Progress photos are never included in cloud backups. You can export your data to a file (JSON / CSV) and import it again from Settings > Your data; the file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

3. Camera, microphone and location

  • Camera is used only when you start it: scanning a food barcode, a meal photo estimate, reading a body-composition printout, and the camera form check / live coach. Images are analysed on your phone and are not stored or sent anywhere. Progress photos are saved only when you take them, in the app's private storage.
  • Microphone is used only while you hold the voice button (workout voice commands, speaking to the coach). Speech is turned into text on your phone when the phone supports on-device recognition or the downloaded AI model can do it; otherwise your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google's) may process the audio under its own policy. Recordings are not stored by KineoFit.
  • Location (precise) is used only while you record a run that you started, to draw the route and measure distance. The route is stored on your phone only. Location is never used for ads by KineoFit.

4. Health data (Health Connect or Huawei Health Kit)

If you allow it, KineoFit reads activity and health data from Health Connect or Huawei Health Kit (for example workouts, steps, distance, heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, weight and body fat) to adapt your training plan, calculate your daily readiness and show your progress. If you turn on each option, KineoFit writes your workouts and runs (and, with Health Connect, your weight, body fat, food energy and water) to Health Connect or Huawei Health Kit so other apps can show them. You can change these permissions at any time in Health Connect or Huawei Health Kit.

Health data read from Health Connect or Huawei Health Kit is processed only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending. The use and transfer of this data follows the Health Connect or Huawei Health Kit permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. On-device AI

Meal-photo estimates and, on some phones, voice recognition run on an AI model that you choose to download (Gemma, from Hugging Face). The download sends no personal data; like any download, the server sees your IP address. After that, the model runs entirely on your phone: your photos and voice are not sent to us or to an AI provider. AI estimates can be wrong and are not medical advice. Coach chat answers come from the app itself, not from this model.

6. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob with Google's User Messaging Platform (consent) in the Google Play version, or Huawei Petal Ads with Huawei's consent tools in the AppGallery version. The ad provider may collect the advertising ID (the Google Advertising ID, or Huawei's OAID), approximate location derived from your IP address, device information and ad interactions to show, measure and protect ads. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree in the consent screen; you can change your choice in Settings. KineoFit never passes your health, nutrition or body data to an ad provider. Buying KineoFit Pro removes all ads.

More information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from partner apps · Ad settings · Huawei Consumer Privacy Statement · Petal Ads Privacy Statement. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads (delete or reset the advertising ID) on Google Play phones, or Settings > Privacy > Ads and privacy (reset the OAID or turn off personalised ads) on Huawei phones.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Google Play Billing or Huawei In-App Purchases (IAP). KineoFit receives only the purchase confirmation needed to unlock Pro; your payment details stay with the store.

8. Other network use

When you scan a barcode that is not in the built-in food list, the app asks Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) for that product, sending only the barcode number. Android's own backup may include KineoFit's small settings file (not your training, nutrition, body data or photos) if backup is on for your phone. On Google Play devices the app also stores the start date of the 14-day AI trial in Google Block Store, so reinstalling the app does not restart the trial. Only that date is stored there. If you turn on heat warnings, the app asks Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for today's weather at the city you picked, sending only that city's coordinates.

Usage and crash reports. To find bugs and see which features are used, the app sends usage events (for example "workout finished", screens opened, purchases made in the app) and crash details to Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google Play) or Huawei AppGallery Connect Analytics Kit and Crash Service (AppGallery), tied to a random ID for this install. These reports never contain health values, measurements, photos or text you typed. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > Your data > Usage and crash reports.

9. Children

KineoFit is meant for people aged 13 and over (16 where local law requires) and asks for your age during setup. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Keeping or deleting your data

Because your data is on your phone, you control it. Settings > Your data > Delete my data lets you:

  • Erase everything on this phone (workouts, meals, body data, photos, models and settings), or delete single entries in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Ask us to delete usage and crash reports already sent: the app opens an email to us with your report ID, and we ask the report service to delete that ID's data within 30 days. You can also reset the report ID, which deletes reports not yet sent and starts a new ID. Without the ID we cannot link any report to you.
  • Delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads (delete or reset the advertising ID) on Google Play phones, or Settings > Privacy > Ads and privacy (reset the OAID or turn off personalised ads) on Huawei phones. Data the ad provider holds is kept under its own policy linked above.

Data written to Health Connect or Huawei Health Kit can be deleted there.

11. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage protected by Android. Network connections made by the app and its ad and purchase services use encryption (HTTPS/TLS). No method is perfectly secure, but we keep data on your device to keep the risk low.

12. Health disclaimer

KineoFit gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Stop if you feel pain and talk to a health professional before major changes.

13. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes are also shown in the app.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: kineofit.support@gmail.com

Delete your data

Steps

  1. Open KineoFit and go to You > Settings > Your data > Delete my data.
  2. Erase all my data deletes everything KineoFit saved on your phone. You can also clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.
  3. Ask us to delete reports already sent opens an email to us with your report ID. You can also write to kineofit.support@gmail.com without the app. We reply and ask the report service to delete that ID's data within 30 days.
  4. Reset my report ID deletes reports not yet sent and starts a new ID.
  5. Open advertising ID settings lets you delete or reset your phone's advertising ID.

What is deleted

  • On your phone: workouts, plans, runs, meals, water, body measurements and scans, progress photos, voice notes, coach chats, downloaded AI models and settings. KineoFit has no account and no servers, so nothing else of yours is kept by us.
  • Usage and crash reports linked to your report ID (Google Firebase on Google Play, Huawei AppGallery Connect on AppGallery). These never contain health values, measurements, photos or text you typed.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Data you copied elsewhere (your own export files, Health Connect or Huawei Health Kit) stays there until you delete it there.
  • Usage reports you do not ask us to delete are kept by the report service for up to 14 months, crash reports for up to 90 days, and then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play or AppGallery under their own rules.
  • Data collected by the ad network (Google AdMob or Huawei Petal Ads) is kept under its own privacy policy, linked in our privacy policy. Deleting your advertising ID stops it being linked to you.
