Privacy Policy for Zadi

Last updated: September 6, 2026

EasyMeasy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by our mobile application, Zadi.

2. Information We Collect

  • Location Data: Zadi collects precise geolocation data when you use the Prayer Times and Qibla features to provide accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. This data is processed locally on your device and is not shared with our own servers.
  • Device Information: We collect information about your device, such as model, operating system, and unique device identifiers (including the Google Advertising ID), to monitor app performance and improve user experience.
  • Usage Data: We collect information on how you interact with the app, such as features used, to help us improve Zadi.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide accurate prayer times and Qibla direction based on your location.
  • Provide personalized advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Improve the app's functionality and user experience.

4. Third-Party Services (AdMob)

Zadi uses Google AdMob for advertising.

  • Google AdMob may collect data — including your Google Advertising ID and, where location-based ads are enabled, approximate location — to provide personalized ads.
  • All data transmitted to Google AdMob is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • You can manage your ad preferences in your device settings (see Section 5 below).
  • Google's Privacy Policy can be viewed at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. How to Manage or Delete Your Advertising Data

You have the right to opt out of personalized advertising and to request deletion of the advertising-related data associated with your device:

  • Delete your Google Advertising ID: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (Android 13 and above) or Opt out of Ads Personalization (earlier versions). This immediately deletes or resets the identifier Google AdMob uses to associate advertising data with your device.
  • Manage or delete data via Google Ad Settings: Visit adssettings.google.com (or Google's My Ad Center at myadcenter.google.com) while signed in to your Google Account to view, manage, or delete advertising data Google has collected, including data collected via AdMob.
  • Disable location-based ads: You can revoke Zadi's location permission at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > Zadi > Permissions > Location.
  • Request manual deletion: For any other data deletion request, contact us at zadi.musllim.app@gmail.com and we will respond to and action your request within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Data Security

We implement administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data, and all data shared with Google AdMob is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Zadi does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately to have it deleted.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.
