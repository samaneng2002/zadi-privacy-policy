# Privacy Policy — Anqa

**Last updated: 23 September 2026**

Anqa is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no login, no friends list and no servers of
its own. Your save file lives on your device and nowhere else.

This policy exists because the game includes Google's advertising and billing services, and
those do collect data. What follows is what they collect, what we collect (almost nothing), and
how to stop it.

---

## What Anqa itself collects

**Nothing.** There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking of any kind written by us.
We do not have a server, so there is nowhere for your data to go even if we wanted it.

Your progress — heroes, levels, gold, settings, language — is stored in a file in the app's own
private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Nobody else can read it, and
neither can we.

## What Anqa asks your device for

| Permission | Why | Can you refuse? |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | One reminder when your idle chest is full | Yes. The game works normally without it, and never asks twice. |
| Internet | Required by the advertising and billing services below | No — Android grants it at install. With no connection the game still plays; ads simply do not load. |

Anqa does **not** ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or phone
number, and does not read anything else on your device.

## What third parties collect

Two Google services are built into the game.

### Google AdMob (advertisements)

Rewarded videos are optional — they only play when you choose to watch one. A rare full-screen
advertisement may appear after collecting idle rewards, and never after a defeat, never during
a battle, never in your first three days, and never again once you have made any purchase.

AdMob collects an advertising identifier and technical information about your device in order
to select and measure adverts. Google's handling of that data is described in their privacy
policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. You can reset or delete your advertising
identifier at any time in **Settings → Privacy → Ads** on your device.

### Google Play Billing (purchases)

If you buy something, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your
card, your name or your address — only a confirmation that a specific product was purchased, so
the game can grant it. Google's terms apply: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

On Huawei devices the same roles are filled by **Huawei Ads Kit** and **Huawei IAP**, under
Huawei's own privacy policy: <https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/>.

## Children

Anqa is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information
from anyone. The game contains optional purchases and optional advertisements, and it is rated
accordingly on each store.

## Your choices

- **Turn off personalised ads** — Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device.
- **Delete everything** — uninstall the app. The save and every setting go with it; there is no
  copy anywhere else.
- **Play without ads entirely** — never tap a rewarded video offer. Nothing in the game is
  gated behind one.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous version stays in
